BattleCraft mod 26.09.26 PRE-ALPHA
Changes since build 24.09.26v5, packs unchanged. The network protocol is unchanged, but update the server and the client together: the fixes work on the server.

- The shop, the airdrop with its caches and loot tables, and the buyback are each edited in one editor: sections on the left, the showcase in the middle the way a player sees it, every property of the chosen thing on the right. Items are dragged from a shelf onto the showcase, tiles and sections are reordered with the mouse, and a new thing is selected at once
- Creating a shop section under a name already taken no longer wipes the old one with its items
- A downed player inside their own base no longer stands up with half a heart when the timer runs out or they give up; /kill works inside a base
- A downed player or a spectator cannot trade in the shop, and shop stock refills at every match
- A kill pays only for an enemy with a team, and no more than once in 90 seconds for the same player
- Point bonus holograms no longer multiply, spectators and dead players cannot start a capture, and joining a capture removes spawn immortality
- A hopper minecart no longer drains a cache, and only a chest or a barrel can be one
- The airdrop does not show its landing spot on the map unless coordinates are announced
- The quarry regenerates mined blocks after a server crash too, and a large drop is given in full
- Item modifiers come off when switched off, and effect levels from several items add up correctly
- Zone rules hold against pistons, item frames, armour stands and chests on minecarts and boats
- Unreadable server files are set aside as .broken instead of being overwritten with empty ones
- The island visualizer shows fades, quiet passages and drops
