BattleCraft mod 05.10.26hotfix PRE-ALPHA
Changes since build 05.10.26, packs unchanged. The network protocol version is the same. The /bc editors need a 05.10.26hotfix server.

- The quarry sits on a map: blocks are dots, green ready, grey regrowing; the chosen one has its own regrowth, teleport and removal without aiming, block kinds are tiles
- Item modifiers are an editor: items as tiles, the first is the one in hand, the current modifiers and the new effect and attribute forms on the right
- Game rules, knockdown, combat timer, map, announcements and the config guard use the editor frame with page descriptions and the module switch
- Things on the editor maps appear, fade and move smoothly
- The separate item picker window is gone, items are taken from the shelf everywhere
- New command quarry at <pos> remove|cooldown set|cooldown reset
