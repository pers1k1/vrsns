BattleCraft mod 15.09.26v6 PRE-ALPHA
Fixes on top of the previous build, the packs are unchanged.

- A base or shop tag in the HUD collapses to its dot until the crosshair is on it, the way a capture point tag does: it no longer covers the view or pushes its neighbours around
- A collapsed tag is easier to spot, its dot is less transparent
- Player markers, team and personal alike, are drawn in the interface accent colour in the world, on the minimap and on the full map
