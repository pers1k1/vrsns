BattleCraft mod 14.09.26hotfix PRE-ALPHA
Only the mod is updated, the client and server packs are unchanged. This build was never played through.

Showcase editor
- The shop panel gained a Showcase row: the shop opens the way a player sees it, on the whole catalogue
- A tile is dragged with the mouse, the neighbours move aside, and the item lands where it was dropped
- In the editor the buy button becomes a remove button, and the selection steps to the neighbour
- A button switches the showcase to what a chosen team is actually offered
- The item model spins and zooms, and the editor stays open outside a shop zone

Capture points
- A point holds a list of commands instead of one: each is shown in full with its own remove button, up to eight

Map
- A label carries its own size: a slider in the panel, a command, and the corner handle after a double click
- Who sees a label is set on the map itself
- Black joined the colour palette
- Every map marker keeps its size relative to the map instead of appearing to shrink as you zoom in

Interface
- Switching a value with the arrows is animated again
- Deleting in a list no longer throws the selection back to the top
