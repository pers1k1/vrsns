BattleCraft mod 15.09.26v7 PRE-ALPHA
Fixes on top of the previous build, the packs are unchanged.

- A world tag now fades out at its range limit instead of vanishing in a single frame
- Marker range is a setting now: capture points, bases and shops, map marks and player markers, 1500 blocks each. The Markers tab in the game rules and the /bc markers commands
- A player marker and an ally position remember the world they were made in, so a ping placed in the nether is no longer drawn in the overworld
- The tag of a removed zone no longer lingers in the world
