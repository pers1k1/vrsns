BattleCraft ModPack & ServerMap - changelog

BattleCraft mod 24.09.26v4 PRE-ALPHA
Changes since build 24.09.26v3, packs unchanged. An old client does not work with the new server: network channels changed version.

- The music visualizer on the island now follows the iPhone: bar colour comes from the blurred cover art, bars are split by frequency (bass on the left), they move in a jump and settle 15 times a second, and loudness is no longer adjusted automatically
- An open menu is no longer shown to other players: the window held in hand and the live frame for teammates are gone
- The config check no longer kicks clients installed from the launcher: personal files (voice chat, graphics, Distant Horizons, the pack's own module configs) are not compared, a file the player lacks is not a mismatch, and an operator stays on the server and gets the list of files instead
- Menus are smoother: row values, toggles and keys change with a slide, the selection in side lists fades out and in, rows glide to their new place, and a server reply no longer resets row animations
- Team select, the map and minimap settings open and close like every other menu, and players fade in and out in team select
- Commands that run other commands as the server (addcommand for /bc and for points) require level 4
- A point buff with an unknown effect is rejected on input and no longer crashes the server; final point bonuses survive a restart
- The final point is not handed to a team that may not take it; a surrender vote counts only online players, and the winner is chosen among teams with players
- Map marks no longer generate chunks, map chunks from clients are not accepted, and team maps are saved safely
- Selling: the currency cannot be sold and an item with a price cannot become the currency; the guard is switched off with /sell currencyguard or the "Currency guard" row in the sell menu
- Immortality ends when the immortal player attacks; void damage goes through it
- Knockdown: only teammates can revive, a downed player deals no damage; /knockdown config rejects values outside the config ranges
- No kill reward for killing yourself
- The no-build rule in zones now covers buckets, flint and steel and fire charges
- Leaving a match no longer duplicates items; the combat timer and the IP lock (ipLock) are kept in config
- Menu fields accept up to 256 characters, a shop item description up to 160, and a command too long to send is not sent from the menu
- The red alarm tint in the HUD is laid over the dimming instead of turning it black; the first-run wizard goes back only on Esc

BattleCraft mod 24.09.26v3 PRE-ALPHA
Changes since build 15.09.26v7, packs unchanged. An old client does not work with the new server: network channels changed version.

- Loot caches: any chest, barrel, furnace or shulker box can become a cache with match loot - filled when the match starts, optionally refilled on a timer or after it is emptied, emptied when the match ends and locked outside a match
- A cache is marked by a quiet shader: a thin frame hugging the block with a light running along its edges, a pool of light on the floor, a ring counting to the next refill and a wave on fill; colour by rarity, a hidden cache does not glow
- A cache cannot be broken, blown up, drained by a hopper or merged with a chest placed next to it
- Loot editor (/airdrop editor): items as tiles, drag to reorder, drag in from the inventory or from all items with search, chance and counts as numbers, the real drop chance and a test roll
- Attachment bench for guns in loot and in the shop: 3D model, slots as cards, attachments as tiles with icons, hover shows what an attachment gives; SuperbWarfare attachments now show on the model
- No more taking items from the hand: kill reward, point reward and income, sell price and currency and new shop items are picked in a window with the inventory, search and a model
- Airdrop: settings from the menu no longer reset, the zone center works, the fall time is honest, a crate lands and despawns far from players, its map marker stays until it is looted, nothing can be put into a crate
- New airdrop settings: drops only during a match, clear crates when the match ends, announce coordinates or leave only the smoke, pick the loot table
- Loot commands now name the table: /airdrop loot <table> ...
- An open menu is visible to others: the player holds a glass window of the open screen, teammates see a live frame in it
- The quarry is calmer, ore tags only up close and in plain sight, the pickaxe wears
- Tags leave the HUD while you stand in their zone; an operator mark can draw its hide zone
- Damage numbers are drawn in the pack's own font under the crosshair
- Every colour is picked with the palette, zones and quarry cells fade in, the selection in world and server lists glides
- Buttons ease between enabled and disabled and swap labels smoothly, menu rows no longer nudge their value when pressed
- Long text scrolls and dissolves at the edge, the island cover and Discord picture are sharp

ModPack 05.09.26hotfix · BattleCraft mod 05.09.26
Pack for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge 47.4.x, Java 17). The unified BattleCraft mod combines the gameplay modules:
- BattleCraft core: match lifecycle, team selection, pack interface, Discord RPC
- AirDrop: scheduled loot drops at set locations
- CapturePoints: team-based point capture
- CombatTimer: combat tag and combat-log protection
- DamageIndicator: damage indicators
- Immortality: spawn invulnerability
- ItemModifiers: commands for tweaking item properties
- KillReward: kill rewards
- KnockDown: downed state, reviving allies and giving up
- MiniMap: minimap and radar with team markers
- QuarryMod: automated resource mining
- SellMod: shop and item selling
- Zones: areas with their own rules, a new module

Sculk integration: Superb Warfare sounds and gunshots trigger sensors and shriekers; a shrieker summons the Warden on the first shriek.

BattleCraft mod: what is new
- An interface instead of commands: everything is gathered in the /bc menu, the commands remain as a fallback
- Glass styling: custom world and server pickers, custom loading screens, hover hints
- Customization: palette, glass style, window motion sets, personal presets
- HUD editor inside the game: blocks are dragged, snapped and resized with the mouse
- Colors picked in the launcher are used by the mod interface
- A first-run wizard walks through the setup in six steps
- Zones: rules for what happens inside an area, including damage, explosions and access, with owner exceptions
- Shop: departments and sections, stock with scheduled restocks, per-team access, vehicle requirements based on ammo and batteries
- Capture points: several capture modes, progress rollback, a cooldown for the initiating team, three ways to split the reward
- Minimap: explored terrain is shared across the team and never handed to the enemy
- Markers: permanent landmarks with per-team visibility and world tags that never overlap
- Survival Instinct adrenaline: a stamina bonus, a racing pulse and an overdose when taken too often
- Stamina: it drains while jumping and is restored in full on respawn
- Match rules: 17 switches, start requirements and module toggles
- Killfeed, interface sound schemes, a custom font

Mod list
Added:
- Canary, Saturn, spark: tick optimization and profiling
- Camera Overhaul, Not Enough Animations: camera and body animations
- ExtraSounds, Falling Leaves, Sodium Dynamic Lights: interface sounds, falling leaves, dynamic lighting
