- Settings and logs are no longer lost when the Documents folder is locked by cloud sync or an antivirus: the launcher moves them to AppData and carries the old config over
- Saving and reading the settings no longer fail silently, and a broken config is kept as a copy next to the new one
- A broken connection is explained in plain words: antivirus HTTPS scanning, a VPN, a proxy or provider blocking, instead of the raw transport stream sentence
- The system check now also shows up on the very first run, before signing in
