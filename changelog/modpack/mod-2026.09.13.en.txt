BattleCraft mod 13.09.26 ALPHA
Only the mod is updated; the client and server packs are unchanged.

Capture points
- A point can run a command when it is taken: a row in /bc, with the point name, the owning team and the coordinates as placeholders
- The capture reward can be switched off with a zero, and the reward and income items are taken from the hand
- Point income and its interval are set from the menu, not only by command
- The owner of a point can be cleared straight from the owner row
- A final point now carries the same settings as an ordinary one

Marks
- A mark can stay on the map only: no tag over the world, while the minimap and the full map keep it
- A mark can be renamed from the menu
- World tags have a size of their own: a slider in the customization screen, from half to one and a half of the stock size

Interface
- The whole capture HUD leaves under the player list with an animation instead of being dropped from the frame
- Panels refresh at once: an added or removed command, a renamed mark and a changed owner are visible without reopening
- The Require teams switch did nothing at all; it works now
- The commands run at match start, at match stop and on surrender are edited in the Automation tab
- The build config check has a screen of its own: take a snapshot, recheck, clear, forget a single path
- Airdrop: loot count, chance and order, duplicate, tag stripping, clearing the table, and the drop centre by coordinates
- Quarry: the cooldown of the block in the crosshair and a list of quarry blocks
- Immortality is granted and taken per player from the menu
- Item modifiers: lore lines, and effects and attributes go into the selected item
- Selling: price and currency by item identifier
- Shop: an item by identifier, and moving an item or a subsection into another section
- Zones: the zone colour and the way back to the team colour
- Game rules reset in one row, and a team is created under any name
