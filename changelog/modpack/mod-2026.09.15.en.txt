BattleCraft mod 15.09.26 PRE-ALPHA
Fixes on top of the previous build, the packs are unchanged.

- Radio transmit moved to Caps Lock: the key is free and falls under the little finger, at the price of Windows flipping the caps state on every press
- The points key moved to the key left of the digits, capture and revive are back on left Alt, goggle zoom sits on Y
- Interacting with a gun in hand moved to mouse button 5, next to melee
- Every key of the pack now lives in one BattleCraft section of the controls screen, and the surrender key is finally named
- Switching colours and glass no longer blinks: the palette travels over half a second, both in first-run setup and in customisation
- The item icon in the shop no longer jitters behind its tile
