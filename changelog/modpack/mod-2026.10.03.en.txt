BattleCraft mod 03.10.26 PRE-ALPHA
Changes since build 28.09.26v4, packs unchanged. The network protocol is unchanged: a 03.10.26 client works with a 28.09.26 server.

- The island moves like the Dynamic Island on an iPhone, timed frame by frame from a screen recording of the phone. It draws in slightly before opening, springs into the card with a light overshoot, and springs back into the pill on its own curve without the draw-in
- The whole island blurs while it moves: the glass edge, what shows through it and the contents. The pill and the card cross over under the blur, nothing disappears
- A track change gives the island a short swell with a bounce
- The main menu island swells under a held mouse button and opens or closes on release
