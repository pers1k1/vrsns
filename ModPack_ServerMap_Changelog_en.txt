BattleCraft ModPack & ServerMap - changelog

ModPack 05.09.26 · BattleCraft mod 05.09.26
Pack for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge 47.4.x, Java 17). The unified BattleCraft mod combines the gameplay modules:
- BattleCraft core: match lifecycle, team selection, pack interface, Discord RPC
- AirDrop: scheduled loot drops at set locations
- CapturePoints: team-based point capture
- CombatTimer: combat tag and combat-log protection
- DamageIndicator: damage indicators
- Immortality: spawn invulnerability
- ItemModifiers: commands for tweaking item properties
- KillReward: kill rewards
- KnockDown: downed state, reviving allies and giving up
- MiniMap: minimap and radar with team markers
- QuarryMod: automated resource mining
- SellMod: shop and item selling
- Zones: areas with their own rules, a new module

Sculk integration: Superb Warfare sounds and gunshots trigger sensors and shriekers; a shrieker summons the Warden on the first shriek.

BattleCraft mod: what is new
- An interface instead of commands: everything is gathered in the /bc menu, the commands remain as a fallback
- Glass styling: custom world and server pickers, custom loading screens, hover hints
- Customization: palette, glass style, window motion sets, personal presets
- HUD editor inside the game: blocks are dragged, snapped and resized with the mouse
- Colors picked in the launcher are used by the mod interface
- A first-run wizard walks through the setup in six steps
- Zones: rules for what happens inside an area, including damage, explosions and access, with owner exceptions
- Shop: departments and sections, stock with scheduled restocks, per-team access, vehicle requirements based on ammo and batteries
- Capture points: several capture modes, progress rollback, a cooldown for the initiating team, three ways to split the reward
- Minimap: explored terrain is shared across the team and never handed to the enemy
- Markers: permanent landmarks with per-team visibility and world tags that never overlap
- Survival Instinct adrenaline: a stamina bonus, a racing pulse and an overdose when taken too often
- Stamina: it drains while jumping and is restored in full on respawn
- Match rules: 17 switches, start requirements and module toggles
- Killfeed, interface sound schemes, a custom font
