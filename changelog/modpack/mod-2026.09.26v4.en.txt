BattleCraft mod 26.09.26v4 PRE-ALPHA
Changes since build 26.09.26v3, packs unchanged. The network protocol is unchanged: a 26.09.26v4 client works with a 26.09.26v3 server.

- In the shop, airdrop and buyback editors a right click on an item, a section, a cache, a table or empty space opens a menu of actions: copy, attachments, move to start or end, delete, new part, rename, move up or down, fill and empty a cache, test roll, change the currency
- The menu unfolds from the cursor and its highlight follows the mouse; deleting a section, a part, a table or a cache asks for a second click, and Esc or a click outside closes it
