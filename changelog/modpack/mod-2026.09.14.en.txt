BattleCraft mod 14.09.26 PRE-ALPHA
Only the mod is updated, the client and server packs are unchanged. This build was never played through: report anything that breaks.

Shop
- Every item carries its own stock scope: shared for the whole server, one per team, or one per player
- A limit like "one every thirty seconds" on a personal stock no longer queues the whole team
- The scope is a row named Stock on the Stock tab of the shop panel

Map
- Labels: text without a plate and without a dot, glowing in its own colour, up to six lines
- The middle mouse button opens a menu of actions: teleport here, a new label, and on a label its text, lines, colour and deletion
- An operator moves a label by double clicking and dragging, and a hovered label lights up
- A label follows the same team visibility as a mark and is edited in the same panel

Capture points
- A point is required or optional: only required points open the final one
- A point can be hidden from the HUD while staying on the map and being captured as usual
- A new switch keeps the final point for the team that opened it

Gear
- Every player carries the IFF device during a match: it cannot be taken off or dropped, it does not fall on death, and it is removed when the match ends
- A device the player bought is left alone
- The rule sits with the combat rules and can be switched off

Fixed
- The /clear command did not touch Curios slots, so worn items survived it
- The shop panel did not notice a price, stock or restock change made elsewhere
- No menu screen rebuilds itself while a row is taking a typed number or a key
