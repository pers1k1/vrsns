BattleCraft mod 15.09.26v4 PRE-ALPHA
Fixes on top of the previous build, the packs are unchanged.

- An attribute no longer sticks to a player: a removed entry went on being applied for the rest of the session, item or no item
- An item modified in hand is now listed in the Items tab; its entries are shown with their slots and can be taken off one by one
- The attribute setting gained a "What the item gets" line: Add 0.05 to movement speed is +50% while Multiply base 0.05 is +5% - same number, different outcome
- An entry written for any slot, on an item held in both hands, no longer raises a duplicate application error
