BattleCraft mod 24.09.26v5 PRE-ALPHA
Changes since build 24.09.26v4, packs unchanged. The network protocol is unchanged: a 24.09.26v5 client works with a 24.09.26v4 server.

- A player in a team is no longer kicked with "Illegal packet received" a few seconds after joining, singleplayer included
- After a disconnect, Back from the server list leads to the title screen instead of the disconnect screen again
- The visualizer no longer follows the player's volume: the bars stand the same height from full volume down to one percent
- The visualizer answers the sound about twice as fast, without stalls, and moves more smoothly
- The island listens to the Windows Media Player app, Telegram, VLC, PotPlayer, MPC, Winamp and other local players; for players Windows knows nothing about, the track name comes from the window title
- A paused Spotify or browser tab no longer holds the island while a local player is playing
- A track without artwork shows the avatar instead of the previous track's cover; artwork the game could not read before is now shown
- A track without a timeline (Telegram) counts its own time and shows the artist instead of "0:00 / 0:00"
