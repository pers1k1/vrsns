BattleCraft mod 14.09.26 PRE-ALPHA
Only the mod is updated, the client and server packs are unchanged. This build was never played through: report anything that breaks.

Shop
- Every item carries its own stock scope: shared for the whole server, one per team, or one per player
- A limit like "one every thirty seconds" on a personal stock no longer queues the whole team
- The scope is a row named Stock on the Stock tab of the shop panel

Map
- Labels: text without a plate and without a dot, glowing in its own colour, up to six lines
- The middle mouse button opens a menu of actions: teleport here, a new label, and on a label its text, lines, colour and deletion

Capture points
- A point is required or optional: only required points open the final one
- A point can be hidden from the HUD while staying on the map and being captured as usual
- A new switch keeps the final point for the team that opened it

Gear
- Every player carries the IFF device during a match: it cannot be taken off or dropped, it does not fall on death, and it is removed when the match ends
- A device the player bought is left alone
- The rule sits with the combat rules and can be switched off

Fixed

- The shop panel did not notice a price, stock or restock change made elsewhere
- No menu screen rebuilds itself while a row is taking a typed number or a key

BattleCraft mod 13.09.26 ALPHA
Only the mod is updated; the client and server packs are unchanged.

Capture points
- A point can run a command when it is taken: a row in /bc, with the point name, the owning team and the coordinates as placeholders
- The capture reward can be switched off with a zero, and the reward and income items are taken from the hand
- Point income and its interval are set from the menu, not only by command
- The owner of a point can be cleared straight from the owner row
- A final point now carries the same settings as an ordinary one

Marks
- A mark can stay on the map only: no tag over the world, while the minimap and the full map keep it
- A mark can be renamed from the menu
- World tags have a size of their own: a slider in the customization screen, from half to one and a half of the stock size

Interface
- The whole capture HUD leaves under the player list with an animation instead of being dropped from the frame
- Panels refresh at once: an added or removed command, a renamed mark and a changed owner are visible without reopening
- The Require teams switch did nothing at all; it works now
- The commands run at match start, at match stop and on surrender are edited in the Automation tab
- The build config check has a screen of its own: take a snapshot, recheck, clear, forget a single path
- Airdrop: loot count, chance and order, duplicate, tag stripping, clearing the table, and the drop centre by coordinates
- Quarry: the cooldown of the block in the crosshair and a list of quarry blocks
- Immortality is granted and taken per player from the menu
- Item modifiers: lore lines, and effects and attributes go into the selected item
- Selling: price and currency by item identifier
- Shop: an item by identifier, and moving an item or a subsection into another section
- Zones: the zone colour and the way back to the team colour
- Game rules reset in one row, and a team is created under any name

ModPack 05.09.26hotfix · BattleCraft mod 05.09.26
Pack for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge 47.4.x, Java 17). The unified BattleCraft mod combines the gameplay modules:
- BattleCraft core: match lifecycle, team selection, pack interface, Discord RPC
- AirDrop: scheduled loot drops at set locations
- CapturePoints: team-based point capture
- CombatTimer: combat tag and combat-log protection
- DamageIndicator: damage indicators
- Immortality: spawn invulnerability
- ItemModifiers: commands for tweaking item properties
- KillReward: kill rewards
- KnockDown: downed state, reviving allies and giving up
- MiniMap: minimap and radar with team markers
- QuarryMod: automated resource mining
- SellMod: shop and item selling
- Zones: areas with their own rules, a new module

Sculk integration: Superb Warfare sounds and gunshots trigger sensors and shriekers; a shrieker summons the Warden on the first shriek.

BattleCraft mod: what is new
- An interface instead of commands: everything is gathered in the /bc menu, the commands remain as a fallback
- Glass styling: custom world and server pickers, custom loading screens, hover hints
- Customization: palette, glass style, window motion sets, personal presets
- HUD editor inside the game: blocks are dragged, snapped and resized with the mouse
- Colors picked in the launcher are used by the mod interface
- A first-run wizard walks through the setup in six steps
- Zones: rules for what happens inside an area, including damage, explosions and access, with owner exceptions
- Shop: departments and sections, stock with scheduled restocks, per-team access, vehicle requirements based on ammo and batteries
- Capture points: several capture modes, progress rollback, a cooldown for the initiating team, three ways to split the reward
- Minimap: explored terrain is shared across the team and never handed to the enemy
- Markers: permanent landmarks with per-team visibility and world tags that never overlap
- Survival Instinct adrenaline: a stamina bonus, a racing pulse and an overdose when taken too often
- Stamina: it drains while jumping and is restored in full on respawn
- Match rules: 17 switches, start requirements and module toggles
- Killfeed, interface sound schemes, a custom font
