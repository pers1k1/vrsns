BattleCraft mod 05.10.26v6 PRE-ALPHA
Changes since build 05.10.26v5, packs unchanged. The capture point and shop network protocol changed: update the server and clients together.

- Silk typeface for everyone: the config switches to it once with its own halo, the setup wizard marks it "Recommended"
- Sale window for shop items: opens N minutes and closes M minutes after the match start, closed outside a match; padlock with a countdown on the tile
- Team treasury: point income goes once into a shared treasury by default, the team takes from it in its own or a neutral shop, nothing can be put in. Income used to be paid in full to every player
- Island avatar choice: Discord, skin head or your own picture
- The HUD keeps the same share of the screen at any resolution and GUI scale
- The lyric syllable glow fades out smoothly instead of cutting off; lyrics search handles "ё" and "(prod. X)"
- In the editors a dragged element no longer vanishes past the window edge, its glass shows the interface beneath, it lifts and lands; section counts show own items and roll their digits
