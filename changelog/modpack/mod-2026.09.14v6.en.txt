BattleCraft mod 14.09.26v6 PRE-ALPHA
Fixes on top of the previous build, the packs are unchanged. Build v5 could not be downloaded - the file in the release was not named the way the launcher looks for it; v6 replaces it.

- The walkie-talkie is visible in the HUD: while someone speaks on your channel a transmission card sits in the right-hand stack - a record dot, who is talking, how long, a signal meter and a running recording trace
- The trace starts at the first word; a tick in it is voice, a dot is silence
- A teammate is named; an opponent on the same channel is not - a shifting set of symbols stands in for the name, and the server never sends it
- Your own transmission takes the top card, and the same trace unrolls next to the microphone badge while you talk
- The trace is driven by the voice chat itself, so music, Discord and game sounds never move it
- Radio voice now goes through the air: a narrow band and clipping, with distance adding hiss, dropouts and muffling - the further away the speaker, the worse they are heard
- The cards, the trace by the microphone, the distortion and the interference strength are all switches and a slider on the Interface tab of Customisation, and the Radio traffic slot drags and resizes like the rest
