12.09.26
- Field of view and mouse sensitivity are written the way Minecraft reads them: the number on the slider matches the one the game shows
- Field of view now defaults to 70
- Entity distance is gone from the settings and always stays at maximum
- The framerate cap is rounded up to whole tens of the monitor refresh rate: 144 Hz gives 150, 101 Hz gives 110; the detected rate is shown in the label
- Recommended settings are applied only to an installation without its own game settings; after that the "Recommended" button sets them
- The smooth lighting checkbox got a clearer name in the Russian interface
